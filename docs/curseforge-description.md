# StatVerdict

**Open your bags. Hover an item. Know if it is worth equipping.**

StatVerdict gives you clear gear-upgrade verdicts for your **Main Spec and your Off Spec** — no SimulationCraft, no Raidbots, no spreadsheets. It is built for players who loot first and sim never: a fast *equip or skip* answer, right where you already look.

If you chase every 0.1% in a sim, this is not meant to replace that. If you want solid decisions close to best practice, without the toolchain, this is for you.

---

## What you get

- **Upgrade arrows in your bags and on tooltips** — for your Main Spec and your Off Spec, with MS / OS labels for gear already in your loadouts.
- **Stat Progress** — how close you are to your stat targets, with live weights that follow what you still need.
- **Best in Slot and Ranked Trinkets** — your goal's lists (Mythic+, Raid or PvP), your progress, and a roomy tooltip with the recommended gems and enchant, plus *where to find* the item (dungeon or raid and boss).
- **Approve** — save bag pieces into a virtual loadout for a spec you are not wearing, and compare against the gear you actually intend to play.

## Guide: Auto, or choose your tier

Stat priorities, Best in Slot lists and stat targets are copied **one to one** from the guides (Icy Veins and u.gg), never tuned or changed.

- **Auto** (the default) follows your own stats: it shows the targets of the next tier you are working toward, and moves you up when you cover about 90% of the current one. The title bar always tells you where you stand — for example *Auto · Tier 2 · 80% to Tier 3*.
- Or pick **Tier 1, Tier 2 or Tier 3** yourself, from comfortable to demanding.
- Best in Slot and trinkets follow your tier, so an easier goal never shows gear only the best-equipped players can have.

## How it decides

StatVerdict does not simulate your DPS. It uses a practical, explainable priority model:

- **Armor and weapons** — item level and your primary stat come first. Crit, Haste, Mastery and Versatility follow your build's order, with live pressure toward what you are still missing.
- **Rings and necks** — they often have no primary stat, so the top secondary is boosted to compare them fairly.
- **Trinkets** — judged from a Best-in-Slot tier list first, because the proc or on-use usually matters more than the stats on the item.
- Ten or more item levels is a clear upgrade; small steps are decided by the stats. A verdict is the same from both sides — two items can never both be better than each other.

## Why StatVerdict

- **Fast** — answers in your bags and tooltips.
- **Two specs** — check Off Spec loot without respeccing.
- **Honest** — a gear coach for real decisions, not a parse optimizer. If its data is missing or out of date, it tells you instead of guessing.
- **Self-contained** — no website required to get a verdict.

*Verdict Points are heuristic, not simulated DPS. Proc and on-use power, encounter mechanics and set-bonus size are outside the model. Guide data is bundled with each release — update the addon regularly to keep it current.*
