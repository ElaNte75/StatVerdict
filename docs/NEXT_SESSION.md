## What the addon is
Retail addon (interface 120100, Lua 5.1) that judges gear for Main Spec and Off Spec: bag/tooltip verdicts and upgrade arrows, a Guide drawer
(Auto / Tier 1-3 stat-target tiers per spec), Best in Slot and ranked trinkets that follow the tier, "Where to find" lines, a Features drawer.
Data in `StatVerdict/Data/Generated/` comes from ClassCodex (Icy Veins PvE, u.gg targets and PvP). `Core/SV_ProfileRepository.lua` reads it,
`Core/SV_Scoring.lua` + `SV_Modifiers.lua` score, `Core/SV_SpecSnapshot.lua` keeps the saved loadouts, `UI/` draws everything.
The evaluation contexts are built once per bag scan (`ns.BeginContextScan` / `EndContextScan` in `Core/SV_EvaluationContext.lua`).
