# StatVerdict 2.1.41

Prepared, not uploaded yet (the owner decides when). Everything fixed from now on goes in here. When it is uploaded, rename this file to `2.1.41_<upload date>.md`.
The text below is what goes into the CurseForge changelog box.

```
2.1.41
New
- Holding Alt now shows the other build for as long as you hold it (with an Off Spec set): the stat ranks and the whole verdict switch to the Off Spec, and letting go brings the Main Spec back. It no longer stays switched. Over an item in your bags Alt is the marking key (Alt-Left-Click / Alt-Right-Click) and does not change the tooltip.
- The verdict now says what the item beats: "Better than <item>: +points" on one line (the words virtual loadout are gone). The gold line under it says "Also an upgrade for Off Spec" (not shown while Alt is held).
- A piece the Catalyst would turn into your Best in Slot set piece now gets a short StatVerdict Warning on its tooltip, even when it is no upgrade as it is: "Catalyst it: Best in Slot +points", with Hold Ctrl to preview. It shows on the piece you wear, on the one in your bags or at a vendor, and on the game's comparison tooltip, so you can see both give the same result.
- Alt-click marking on bag items is now two independent switches: Alt-Right-Click switches the Main Spec mark (MS, top right) on or off, Alt-Left-Click the Off Spec mark (OS, top right). A piece marked for both specs shows a gold ring with two arrows instead of MS and OS. Before, an item saved in both loadouts only showed MS, which made the clicks look wrong.
- A bag item you saved in a loadout now says it in one line: "Saved in MS Frost loadout" (MS or OS in gold, the spec in its class colour), or "Saved in MS and OS loadouts" (MS and OS in gold) when it is in both. The spec name is no longer shown twice.
- The Options are grouped by where they show: Window, Bag items, Item tooltips, Character info (new), Best in Slot, Ranked Trinkets. Stat Ranks and the new choices for the character info window no longer sit under Bag items.
- Character info (the pieces you wear): marks on the icons: a gold BIS on a piece in the Best in Slot list, a gold CAT on a piece the Catalyst would make Best in Slot, and a thin gold ring with two arrows on a piece that is also in your other set (the letters sit inside the ring when both are true). Switch them off in Options (Marks on the character sheet).
- The tooltip of a piece you wear is no longer compared with anything. When the piece has a mark, a short StatVerdict info says what it means (Best in Slot, the Catalyst, or also part of your Main or Off Spec set). Switch it off in Options (Info on worn pieces).
Fixed
- Marking a second ring, trinket or one-hand weapon no longer pushes the first one you marked out of the loadout (both went for the same slot); each piece now takes its own slot.
- Switching spec with a marked ring, trinket or one-hand weapon in your bags: it now takes the place of the worn piece that is not in that spec's loadout, never a piece the loadout keeps (before, it went to the slot number saved when you marked it, which could remove the ring both specs share).
- The Catalyst set piece was judged and previewed at the wrong item level (its base level, for example 219 instead of 292), so a good conversion could say "Not better after the Catalyst" and the Ctrl preview showed a much weaker piece. It is now built at the same item level as the item you hold the mouse over.
- The game's comparison tooltip (the piece you wear, next to the one under the mouse) was not recognised by StatVerdict, so it said nothing about the Catalyst. It now says the same as the other piece when both would give the same result.
- Game data updated: the guides' Best in Slot lists, stat priorities and item upgrade levels are refreshed to the latest.
```
