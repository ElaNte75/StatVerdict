# StatVerdict 2.1.41

Prepared, not uploaded yet (the owner decides when). Everything fixed from now on goes in here. When it is uploaded, rename this file to `2.1.41_<upload date>.md`.
The text below is what goes into the CurseForge changelog box.

```
2.1.41
New
- Holding Alt now shows the other build for as long as you hold it (with an Off Spec set): the stat ranks and the whole verdict switch to the Off Spec, and letting go brings the Main Spec back. It no longer stays switched. Over an item in your bags Alt is the marking key (Alt-Left-Click / Alt-Right-Click) and does not change the tooltip.
- The verdict now says what the item beats: "Better than <item>: +points" on one line (the words virtual loadout are gone). The gold line under it says "Also an upgrade for Off Spec" (not shown while Alt is held).
- A piece the Catalyst would turn into your Best in Slot set piece now gets a short StatVerdict Warning on its tooltip, even when it is no upgrade as it is: "Catalyst it: Best in Slot +points", with Hold Ctrl to preview. It shows on the piece you wear, on the one in your bags or at a vendor, and on the game's comparison tooltip, so you can see both give the same result.
- Alt-click marking on bag items is now two independent switches: Alt-Right-Click switches the Main Spec mark (MS, top right) on or off, Alt-Left-Click the Off Spec mark (OS, top right). A piece marked for both specs shows a gold ring with two arrows instead of MS and OS. Before, an item saved in both loadouts only showed MS, which made the clicks look wrong.
- A bag item you saved in a loadout now says it in one line: "Saved in MS Frost loadout" (MS or OS in gold, the spec in its class colour), or "Saved in MS and OS loadouts" (MS and OS in gold) when it is in both. The spec name is no longer shown twice.
- The Options are grouped by where they show: Window, Bag items, Item tooltips, Character info (new), Best in Slot, Ranked Trinkets. Stat Ranks and the new choices for the character info window no longer sit under Bag items.
- Character info (the pieces you wear): marks on the icons: a gold BIS on a piece in the Best in Slot list, a gold CAT on a piece the Catalyst would make Best in Slot, and a thin gold ring with two arrows on a piece that is also in your other set (the letters sit inside the ring when both are true). Switch them off in Options (Marks on the character sheet).
- The tooltip of a piece you wear is no longer compared with anything. When the piece has a mark, a short StatVerdict info says what it means (Best in Slot, the Catalyst, or also part of your Main or Off Spec set). Switch it off in Options (Info on worn pieces).
- Auto mark (Options > Bag items, on by default): every piece you wear is marked for the spec you play, so it is put on again when you come back to that spec; a piece you replace loses the mark for that spec (it keeps the other build's mark). Alt-Click on an item in your bags (either mouse button) saves it for the build you are not playing, and for the build you play too when it is an upgrade for both (it then shows the gold ring); click again to take it out. A mark you make by hand stays until you wear that piece or another one in its slot. With Auto mark off, Alt-Right-Click (Main Spec) and Alt-Left-Click (Off Spec) work as before.
- When you put on a piece and it is an upgrade for your other build too, it is saved for that build as well (the gold ring); a mark you made by hand there is left alone.
- Both builds are compared with their loadout, always. A build you choose in StatVerdict, even a specialization you have never played, gets a loadout the moment you choose it: a copy of what you wear now, with your current stats (an approximation until you play that spec once). For the spec you play, a piece you marked for a slot that is waiting in your bags counts as what the slot has.
- A piece made by the Catalyst keeps the marks of the piece it was made from, in both builds (the gold ring stays), when it appears as the old one disappears and it fits the same slot.
- Marks now know their piece by the game's own serial number. Two copies of the same item are told apart, and a gem, an enchant or an upgrade no longer makes a marked piece look like a different one. Marks made before keep working by item.
- Right-click on a ring, trinket or one-hand weapon in your bags now puts it where the tooltip says ("Better than <piece>"): the piece the tooltip names is the one replaced, and nothing else moves. Before, the game chose the slot by itself, which was not always the piece the tooltip named.
- Character info: a red arrow pointing down in the top right corner of a worn piece when a piece in your bags is better for the spec you play. An empty slot never gets it. Hover the arrow or the piece to see which one; click the arrow when wearing it is your choice (it comes back if you wear the piece again later). While the arrow shows, the gold ring is not shown on that piece. Switch it off in Options (Character info, Arrow on worn pieces).
- The tooltip of a worn piece that belongs to one build only says which one: "Part of your MS Frost set" (information only, nothing is drawn on the icon).
- A bag item saved in both builds now shows one line (Saved in MS and OS loadouts) and nothing is compared. An item saved in one build only is still judged for the other build, with one plain line under the verdict: "Also saved in OS Blood loadout", and its bag mark keeps the green arrow next to the MS or OS letter. Two copies of one item are told apart.
- The CAT mark on the character info icons is drawn with thicker letters, so it is seen at once.
Fixed
- Marking a second ring, trinket or one-hand weapon no longer pushes the first one you marked out of the loadout (both went for the same slot); each piece now takes its own slot.
- Switching spec with a marked ring, trinket or one-hand weapon in your bags: it now takes the place of the worn piece that is not in that spec's loadout, never a piece the loadout keeps (before, it went to the slot number saved when you marked it, which could remove the ring both specs share).
- The Catalyst set piece was judged and previewed at the wrong item level (its base level, for example 219 instead of 292), so a good conversion could say "Not better after the Catalyst" and the Ctrl preview showed a much weaker piece. It is now built at the same item level as the item you hold the mouse over.
- The game's comparison tooltip (the piece you wear, next to the one under the mouse) was not recognised by StatVerdict, so it said nothing about the Catalyst. It now says the same as the other piece when both would give the same result.
- A marked ring, trinket or other piece in your bags was sometimes not put on when you switched specialization, because the game writes the specialization into an item's link and the saved piece looked different from the one in your bags. Marks are now matched by the piece itself.
- The MS / OS marks on bag items now appear as soon as the bags open, and no longer stay hidden for good after you close and reopen the bags quickly.
- The marks on the character info icons now follow the loadouts right after you change gear (they could be drawn a moment too early and stay out of date).
- Less work after a gear or specialization change: when nothing in your loadout or stats changed, StatVerdict no longer redraws the bags, the tooltips and the window three times.
- An upgrade can give a piece a new serial number, and its marks (the MS / OS marks, the gold ring) used to be lost. When a marked piece is gone and the same item is there with a new number, StatVerdict now takes it for the same piece and moves the marks of both builds to it.
- A piece that sat in your bags for a build (the OS or MS mark) could look like "nothing" while you played the other spec: no mark, no tooltip line. A piece could be marked in both ring or trinket slots at once, which made the real piece of the slot look like another copy. A piece can now be marked in one slot only, and old doubles are repaired by themselves.
- A mark made for a spec you were not playing now lets go as soon as you wear another piece in that slot (it used to hold the slot for good).
- The marks catch up when a fight ends and after you take a mark away with Alt-Click, instead of waiting for the next gear change.
- Smoother game: the StatVerdict window is rebuilt once per burst of stat events (it was rebuilt for every event, up to 80 ms at a time), and the marks on the character sheet are not worked out while the sheet is closed.
- Game data updated: the guides' Best in Slot lists, stat priorities and item upgrade levels are refreshed to the latest.
```
